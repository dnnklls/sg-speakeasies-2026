# Singapore Speakeasies – Date Night Picks

A quick list of good speakeasy-style bars in Singapore that work well after dinner for drinks and conversation.

---

## Shortlist – Speakeasies (cocktail-focused)

### 28 HongKong Street (Boat Quay / CBD)
- **Vibe:** Classic, dark, unmarked-door speakeasy. Award-winning cocktails.
- **Good for:** Safe, reliable “I planned this” option.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=28+HongKong+Street+Singapore+059667)

### Cat Bite Club (Duxton)
- **Vibe:** Hidden behind Monument Coffee. Agave-focused (tequila/mezcal etc.).
- **Good for:** If she likes margaritas / mezcal or you want something niche.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cat+Bite+Club+75+Duxton+Road+Singapore+089534)

### Junior The Pocket Bar (Ann Siang)
- **Vibe:** Tiny (around 10 seats), themed rotating menu, very intimate.
- **Good for:** Close conversation, cozy date energy.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Junior+The+Pocket+Bar+6+Ann+Siang+Hill+Singapore+069787)

### Night Hawk (Tanjong Pagar)
- **Vibe:** Sleek, slightly futuristic, very intentional cocktails.
- **Good for:** Stylish, slightly more designed experience.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Night+Hawk+bar+Singapore)

### The Other Room (Orchard / Marriott Tang Plaza)
- **Vibe:** Hidden behind curtains in a hotel, barrel-aged spirits, plush.
- **Good for:** If dinner is in Orchard / town; feels like a hotel speakeasy.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Other+Room+320+Orchard+Road+Singapore+238865)

### Taylor Adam (Raffles Place)
- **Vibe:** Fake tailor shop front; you enter through a “fitting room”.
- **Good for:** Playful reveal but still sophisticated.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://goo.gl/maps/8NpRbBAPmZmXef6F6)

### Sago House (Chinatown)
- **Vibe:** Shophouse top floor, weekly changing menu, award-winning.
- **Good for:** Cocktail nerd cred with casual vibe.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sago+House+Singapore)

### The Secret Mermaid (Raffles Place / CBD)
- **Vibe:** Hidden in an underground passage, focuses on American craft spirits.
- **Good for:** Post-dinner drinks if you’re already in the CBD.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Secret+Mermaid+10+Collyer+Quay+B1-09+Ocean+Financial+Centre+Singapore+049315)

### Mama Diam (Prinsep)
- **Vibe:** Mama shop façade with hidden bar. Nostalgic, fun, IG-friendly.
- **Good for:** Playful, slightly louder energy.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mama+Diam+Singapore)

### The Hidden Story (Katong)
- **Vibe:** Behind a “meat freezer” door in a steakhouse, Peranakan-inspired drinks.
- **Good for:** If you’re doing dinner in the East and want to stay nearby.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Hidden+Story+Katong+Singapore)

## Craft Beer / IPA Bars (easier to drink at)

### American Taproom (City Hall / Bugis side)
- **Vibe:** Casual, noisy, very beer-focused. Big screens, proper bar energy.
- **Beer:** 30+ rotating taps with lots of IPAs, lagers, stouts.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=American+Taproom+Singapore)

### Lion Brewery Co Taproom (CBD)
- **Vibe:** Smart casual, works for dates and groups.
- **Beer:** Their own beers on tap (including The Boss IPA) plus guests.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lion+Brewery+Co+22+%23b1-03+Singapore+049814)

### Smith Street Taps (Chinatown Complex)
- **Vibe:** Hawker centre chaos but with serious beer; very local and chill.
- **Beer:** Rotating taps with local and international craft (often IPAs).
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Smith+Street+Taps+Chinatown+Complex+Singapore)

### Ziggy Zaggy (Bukit Timah)
- **Vibe:** Neighborhood bar, American comfort food.
- **Beer:** Strong IPA list on tap and bottle.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ziggy+Zaggy+Singapore)

### TAP Craft Beer Bar (Orchard / One Raffles Link / Boat Quay)
- **Vibe:** Multiple outlets; casual standing/sitting bar.
- **Beer:** 20+ taps, lots of IPAs and easy lagers.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=TAP+Craft+Beer+Bar+Singapore)

### Orh Gao Taproom (Botanic Gardens)
- **Vibe:** Chill, slightly hipster kopitiam energy.
- **Beer:** Rotating taps, often hoppy stuff + sours.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Orh+Gao+Taproom+Singapore)

### Almost Famous Craft Beer Bar (CHIJMES)
- **Vibe:** Minimalist, indoor, CHIJMES courtyard outside.
- **Beer:** 16 taps of regional + international craft.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Almost+Famous+Craft+Beer+Bar+Singapore)

### LeVel33 (Marina Bay)
- **Vibe:** Microbrewery in the sky; view of Marina Bay.
- **Beer:** Their own IPA plus other house brews.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=LeVel33+8+Marina+Boulevard+Singapore)
