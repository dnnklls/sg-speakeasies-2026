Casual spots where you can **eat properly and drink** without moving venue.
Think **Naga House energy**: designed but not pretentious, chill, enough food.

## Food-forward
*For when you want a real meal and can stay to drink after.*

### Mizue — cozy Japanese cafe / bar (Club Street)
- **Food heaviness:** Medium–heavy (small plates you can turn into a full meal)
- **Best for:** Slightly nicer first/second date in town, sharing a few plates + wine/cocktails.
- **Why pick this:** Feels like a grown-up cousin of Naga House — Japanese-ish, intimate, designed but still casual.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mizue+Club+Street+Singapore)

---

### Ne Ne Neko — Japanese cafe → izakaya (PLQ / Paya Lebar)
- **Food heaviness:** Heavy (donburi, noodles, izakaya plates).
- **Best for:** One-stop dinner + drinks if you’re okay being in the East.
- **Why pick this:** Same “day cafe / night bar” idea as Naga House but with more proper mains.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ne+Ne+Neko+PLQ+Singapore)

---

### Nickeldime Drafthouse — pizza + craft beer (Novena)
- **Food heaviness:** Heavy (pizza, burgers, bar mains).
- **Best for:** Casual, eat-a-lot night where beer is the main drink.
- **Why pick this:** More gastropub than Naga House, but easy “sit for hours, eat and drink in one place.”
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Nickeldime+Drafthouse+Singapore)

## Drink-forward
*For when drinks are the main event, but food is still real enough to count as dinner.*

### Naga House — your baseline (Lor Telok)
- **Food heaviness:** Medium (sandwiches, bowls, fried bits).
- **Best for:** After-work into evening, a bit of everything: beers, cocktails, and snacky-but-filling food.
- **Why pick this:** You already know the vibe; good fallback if nothing else feels right.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Naga+House+14+Lor+Telok+Singapore)

---

### Toku Nori — handroll bar with drinks (Telok Ayer)
- **Food heaviness:** Medium (sushi handrolls + sides as a full meal).
- **Best for:** Sushi-focused date with sake/cocktails, staying put at one counter.
- **Why pick this:** Modern Japanese, compact space, feels a bit more polished than Naga House but still relaxed.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Toku+Nori+Singapore)

---

### Common Man Stan — cafe → wine/bar (Stanley Street, CBD)
- **Food heaviness:** Medium–heavy (brunch-style mains, plates you can treat as dinner).
- **Best for:** Central, low-risk choice when you want decent food and drinks without strong “bar” energy.
- **Why pick this:** Familiar cafe feel plus proper drinks later; easy to sit and talk.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Common+Man+Stan+Singapore)

## Late night
*For later starts or when you might end up eating closer to 9–10pm.*

### Grids Coffee Bar — indie cafe / bar (Chinatown)
- **Food heaviness:** Medium (Japanese-ish comfort food + bar snacks).
- **Best for:** Slightly artsy, low-key night where you don’t want a crowd.
- **Why pick this:** Multi-concept shophouse, good for lingering over food + drinks if you start a bit later.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Grids+Coffee+Bar+Singapore)

---

### Lion Brewery Co Taproom — smart-casual brewery (CBD)
- **Food heaviness:** Heavy (full food menu).
- **Best for:** Later dinner with craft beer in a nicer-feeling space.
- **Why pick this:** If you want something a step up from a pub but still relaxed, with good beer and enough food.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lion+Brewery+Co+22+%23b1-03+Singapore+049814)

## How to Use This (Fast)

- **Don’t overthink it.**
- If you want **closest to Naga House but slightly nicer / more food** → start with **Mizue** or **Toku Nori**.
- If you want **heavier food and beer** → look at **Nickeldime** or **Lion Brewery Co**.
- Tap the **Open in Google Maps** link for the one that feels right and go.
