# Singapore Date Night Spots – Eat & Drink in One Place

You want **one spot** where you can eat properly and drink, with vibes like **Naga House**:
casual, a bit designed, chill, not too clubby.

All entries below have **Open in Google Maps** links.

---

## 1️⃣ Main Picks – Naga House Energy, Slightly Nicer & More Food

These are the first places to think about.

### Mizue (Club Street)
- **Why:** Japanese kissaten-style cafe by day, wine/cocktail bar at night. Cozy, intimate, feels designed but still relaxed.
- **Food:** Japanese-Italian small plates and bites that actually work as a proper meal if you share a few.
- **Vibe level:** Naga House → a bit more grown-up, still casual.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mizue+Club+Street+Singapore)

### Ne Ne Neko (PLQ / Paya Lebar)
- **Why:** Japanese café by day, izakaya by night. If you want donburi/noodles then snacks with drinks without moving venue.
- **Food:** Gyu don, somen, izakaya plates. Easy to treat as dinner + drinks in one.
- **Vibe level:** Casual, cute, Japanese-ish, not intimidating.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ne+Ne+Neko+PLQ+Singapore)

### Common Man Stan (Stanley Street, CBD)
- **Why:** Coffee/brunch cafe by day, natural-wine/bar vibes at night. Good if you want something central and familiar-feeling.
- **Food:** Brunch-style mains and plates; you can definitely eat properly here.
- **Vibe level:** Comfortable, slightly trendy, safe choice.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Common+Man+Stan+Singapore)

### Grids Coffee Bar (Chinatown)
- **Why:** Multi-concept (coffee + bar + gallery) in a shophouse. Indie/creative vibe, good for hanging out.
- **Food:** Japanese-ish comfort food and cafe plates plus bar snacks.
- **Vibe level:** Slightly artsy, relaxed, feels like something you “found”.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Grids+Coffee+Bar+Singapore)

### Toku Nori (Telok Ayer)
- **Why:** Handroll bar with sake and cocktails. Good if you want sushi + alcohol in a compact, modern space.
- **Food:** Handrolls and Japanese plates – easy to make this the full dinner.
- **Vibe level:** A bit more polished, still not stiff.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Toku+Nori+Singapore)

### Nickeldime Drafthouse (Novena)
- **Why:** Gastropub style – pizza, mains, proper craft beer. Very easy place to sit for hours.
- **Food:** Heavy enough: pizza, burgers, bar food.
- **Vibe level:** More pub than Naga House, but good if you want casual + heavy food + beer.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Nickeldime+Drafthouse+Singapore)

## 2️⃣ Naga House – Your Baseline

### Naga House (Lor Telok)
- **Why:** Your reference point – Japanese-inspired cafe-by-day, bar-by-night with brutalist/retro interior.
- **Food:** Sandos, gohan bowls, fried chicken, tater tots – snacky but filling.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Naga+House+14+Lor+Telok+Singapore)

## 3️⃣ Japanese-Inspired Bars (If You Want to Level Up the Vibes)

These are more about **drinks + aesthetics**; food is secondary but still present.

### Pop City x Pony (Cecil Street)
- **Why:** Japanese City Pop–inspired cocktail bar by Jigger & Pony. Very designed, fun, good for impressing.
- **Food:** Bar snacks / lighter bites – check menu if you need a full meal.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Pop+City+X+Pony+Singapore)

### Live Twice (Bukit Pasoh)
- **Why:** 1960s Tokyo speakeasy aesthetic, beautiful cocktails.
- **Food:** Limited, better if dinner is elsewhere and you come here after.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Live+Twice+Singapore)

### TAAN Iza-Bar (Bugis area)
- **Why:** Izakaya-style bar, skewers + drinks, Japanese pub energy.
- **Food:** Grilled skewers and izakaya plates – can be dinner if you order enough.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=TAAN+Iza-Bar+Singapore)

## 4️⃣ Craft Beer / IPA Bars with Food (If You Want Beer-Heavy)

### American Taproom (City Hall / Bugis)
- **Why:** Huge beer list, casual, good for people who like choice.
- **Food:** Bar food / mains – fine for a one-stop night.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=American+Taproom+Singapore)

### Lion Brewery Co Taproom (CBD)
- **Why:** In-house beers, smart-casual room, works as a date spot.
- **Food:** Full food menu.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lion+Brewery+Co+22+%23b1-03+Singapore+049814)

### Good Luck Beerhouse (Haji Lane)
- **Why:** Craft beer plus Haji Lane atmosphere; food from nearby spots.
- **Food:** Lighter, more bar + surrounding options than a pure restaurant.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Good+Luck+Beerhouse+Singapore)

### Little Island Brewing Co (Changi Village)
- **Why:** If you’re okay going out East, this is chill and semi-outdoor.
- **Food:** Full food menu with heavier dishes.
- **Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Little+Island+Brewing+Co+Singapore)
